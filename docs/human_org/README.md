# Human Strategic Layer

Welcome to the **Human Strategic Layer** of **KalaGato (Human Data SG Pte. Ltd.)**. This portal serves as the primary operational manual, role mapping, and standard operating procedures (SOPs) for the real-world human team members who govern, steer, and grow our global application portfolio.

KalaGato is a Singapore-registered mobile app portfolio company with its operational heart in India. We build, acquire, and scale utility, security, entertainment, and productivity apps that serve millions of users globally.



## Core Mission & Operating Principles

Our mission is simple yet ambitious: **Build delightful mobile products that solve real everyday problems and scale them to millions.** We are a lean, elite team of high-performing individuals who manage a massive portfolio of live applications. To make this possible, we operate under six fundamental culture and operating principles:

1. **Own Your Domain**: Everyone leads their area. We do not micro-manage; trust is our default. You were hired because you are exceptional at what you do.
2. **Ship, Then Iterate**: We prefer a live product over a perfect plan. Get your work out, measure it, and improve it fast.
3. **Data Over Gut**: Every major decision is backed by metrics. Intuition is valuable, but it must be informed by data, not guesses.
4. **User Obsession**: Our users are in the millions. Their reviews, store ratizngs, and session data guide everything we build.
5. **Async First**: We document decisions thoroughly in Notion, communicate actively on Slack, and keep face-to-face meetings lean and purposeful.
6. **Builder Mindset**: We are a small team managing a big portfolio. Everyone is expected to build, execute, and make a direct impact, not just manage.

## Day-to-Day Operational Rhythms

To coordinate effectively across departments while preserving productive deep-work time, we maintain structured, async-first rhythms:

* **Weekly Team Sync (Monday, 10:00 AM)**: Our primary alignment meeting. The full team reviews previous week's metrics, addresses critical blockers, and aligns on top-level priorities for the upcoming week. This sync is strictly time-boxed to **45 minutes**.
* **Daily Standup**: Check-in on Jira and Slack to flag immediate blockers, collaborate on design or tech bugs, and maintain alignment on sprint deliverables.
* **App Review Triage (Ongoing & Async)**: App owners are responsible for actively monitoring Play Store and App Store reviews. Critical bugs, billing issues, or user pain-points are immediately flagged in the `#app-reviews` Slack channel.
* **Async Sprint Updates (Friday)**: Post a concise 3-line update in your team's Jira/Notion dashboard: *Done / In Progress / Blocked*. No synchronous meeting is required.
