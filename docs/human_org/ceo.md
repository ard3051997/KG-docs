# Founder & Chief Executive Officer (CEO)

The **Founder & Chief Executive Officer (CEO)** holds the ultimate executive authority at KalaGato. This role is responsible for top-level capital allocation, macroeconomic growth strategy, investor relations, mergers and acquisitions (M&A) final decisions, and high-level governance of the KalaGato portfolio.

---

## Core Responsibilities

1. **Strategic Capital Allocation**: Determining the distribution of capital between user acquisition (UA) budgets, research and development, operational overhead, and prospective mobile asset acquisitions.
2. **M&A Portfolio Growth**: Defining the investment thesis and targets for expansion. Managing our **$500,000+ M&A capital allocation** pool to acquire 23 new mobile utility, security, or entertainment applications annually at **1.52x profit multiples**.
3. **Governance & Brand Strategy**: Maintaining relationships with store representatives (Apple App Store & Google Play Console) and driving high-level policy compliance.
4. **Deal Approvals**: Reviewing and signing off on all final deal terms, Asset Purchase Agreements (APA), and large-scale partnership integrations.

---

## Weekly & Monthly Routine

### Weekly Tasks
* **Monetization & Health Audit**: Review primary analytics and revenue data (DAU, MAU, MRR, Gross Margin) to ensure the portfolio is tracking toward our **$300,000 MRR 2026 milestone**.
* **M&A Pipeline Alignment**: Coordinate with the **M&A Analyst** to review scouted mobile app targets, screen due diligence results, and direct negotiating bounds.
* **Monday Weekly Sync**: Lead the weekly sync at 10:00 AM to review team metrics, resolve high-priority blockers, and align on upcoming sprint deliverables.

### Monthly Tasks
* **Budget Strategy Calibration**: Re-evaluate UA budgets, perform cost reduction checks, and check paid UA performance to avoid organic search cannibalization.
* **Stakeholder Reporting**: Formally report overall portfolio health and financial growth to investors and advisory board members.
* **Strategic Reviews**: Align with the **Product Lead** on product roadmaps, paywall discount strategies, and organic video pipeline results.

---

## Mandatory Approval Gates

The Founder & CEO is the exclusive signer for the following actions:

| Category | Trigger Event | Action Required |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Financial Sign-off** | Any budget expansion, licensing, or vendor contract exceeding **10,000** | Formal signature and manual verification |
| **Asset M&A** | Submitting a formal Letter of Intent (LOI) or signing an Asset Purchase Agreement (APA) | Final contract sign-off and escrow authorization |
| **UA Budget Escalations** | Single paid channel budget changes exceeding **$500/day** or portfolio shifts > **$5,000/week** | PM & ASO Lead coordination review and CEO sign-off |
| **Platform Legal Audits** | Addressing high-priority developer policy notices or regulatory filings | Multi-factor console authentication and legal verification |
