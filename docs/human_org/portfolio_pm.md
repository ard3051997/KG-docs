# Product Lead & Portfolio Product Manager (PM)

The **Product Lead & Portfolio Product Manager (PM)** owns the product roadmap, user experience, feature specification, and release coordination for assigned apps within the KalaGato portfolio. This role acts as the primary hub connecting Design, Engineering, Marketing, ASO, and Monetization teams to translate strategic goals into high-converting mobile experiences.

---

## Core Responsibilities

1. **End-to-End Product Roadmaps**: Authoring, maintaining, and prioritizing product backlogs to maximize lifetime value (LTV), subscription conversions, and retention.
2. **PRD Writing & Coordination**: Creating comprehensive **Product Requirement Documents (PRDs)** that cover Design briefs, Marketing guidelines, Monetization structures, technical frontend/backend specifications, tracking events, and rollback plans.
3. **Release Management**: Leading the release process by coordinate design reviews, engineering deliverables, QA signing tests, and rollout percentage emails.
4. **Data-Driven Analysis**: Monitoring operational dashboards (Firebase, AdMob, RevenueCat, Superwall) to track DAU/MAU, paywall conversions, ad click efficacy, and ASO trends.
5. **App Review Triage**: Actively triaging and responding to user ratings and feedback on the App Store and Google Play Console in `#app-reviews`.

---

## Daily & Weekly Routine

### Daily Checklist
- [ ] **Daily Dashboard Audit**: Check active user metrics, real-time crash rates, and billing performance.
- [ ] **Jira Sprint Triage**: Monitor task progress, unblock developers or QA, and verify design asset delivery.
- [ ] **User Review Triage**: Scan latest reviews, reply to negative feedback, and raise tickets for new bug reports.
- [ ] **Async Coordination**: Review and comment on specs in Notion and collaborate in Slack.

### Weekly Checklist
- [ ] **Monday Weekly Sync**: Present previous week's metrics, highlight release blockers, and align on upcoming sprint priorities.
- [ ] **Release Planning Sprints**: Review upcoming candidate builds with the Tech Lead and ASO Lead to ensure readiness.
- [ ] **Analytics Deep-Dive**: Evaluate paywall conversion cohorts and coordinate with the Monetization Lead to optimize floor configurations.

---

## Mandatory Approval Gates & Bounds

The Product Lead manages the following limits and triggers:

| Gate Type | Parameter Boundaries | Required PM Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Budget Allocations** | Any expenditure or external commitment exceeding **10,000** | Formal alignment review and escalation to CEO for approval |
| **Cross-Dept Impact** | Features impacting multiple active apps or central servers | Hold a design-tech sync and verify in PRD impact section |
| **Release Rollouts** | Initiating staging rollouts on Play Store or App Store Connect | Complete release checklist, confirm QA signature, and email stakeholders |
| **Paywall Modifications** | Changing pricing structures or staging paywall experiments | Coordinate with Monetization Lead and verify RevenueCat rules |
