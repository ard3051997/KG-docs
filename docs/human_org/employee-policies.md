# Corporate Employee & Operational Policies

This document establishes the official employee policies, financial thresholds, reimbursement guidelines, and performance review cycles for all personnel at **KalaGato (Human Data SG Pte. Ltd.)**.

---

## 1. Time-Off & Leaves Policy

We respect your personal time and operate a highly flexible, asynchronous-friendly leave policy. The key is early, proactive communication to ensure operational continuity.

### Leave Categories
1. **Casual Leave (12 Days per Annum)**: For personal errands, short breaks, and family matters. A maximum of **3 consecutive casual leave days** can be taken at a time.
2. **Sick Leave (6 Days per Annum)**: For sudden illness or medical emergencies. Simply notify your immediate manager on the day itself before **9:00 AM**.
3. **Public Holidays**: Observed in accordance with the official government holiday calendar of your contract region.

### Planned Leave Application Process
All planned leaves must follow this sequential process:
1. **Slack Notification**: Message your immediate manager on Slack with the requested dates and a brief reason.
2. **Management Approval**: The manager will provide explicit approval via a Slack response.
3. **Shared Calendar Entry**: Add the approved dates to the shared **KalaGato Leaves** Google Calendar.
4. **Compliance Recording**: Submit the request in the Razorpay portal for formal compliance.
5. **Notice Period**: For any planned time-off, you must notify your lead at least **7 working days** in advance.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Operational Coverage**: Because we operate as a highly focused, lean team, coverage is crucial. You must coordinate with your department colleagues to ensure your active projects and weekly deliverables are cleanly handed off before your leave starts.

## 2. Expense & Financial Approvals

KalaGato maintains strict financial discipline to preserve our high blended gross margins of **~90%**. All operational expenses must follow the authorization matrix below:

### Approval Thresholds
* **Expenses Under INR 10,000**: Must be approved in writing by the direct **Department Manager / Lead**.
* **Expenses Exceeding INR 10,000**: Requires formal signature and manual verification from the **Founder & CEO** before any purchasing action or commitment is made.
* **Paid User Acquisition Limits**: Single paid channel budget changes exceeding **$500/day** or portfolio shifts exceeding **$5,000/week** require full product and marketing lead review and final signature by the **Founder & CEO**.

---

## 3. Reimbursement Protocols

Reimbursements are generally not needed as core software and services are provisioned through corporate accounts. However, in specific approved instances:

* **Pre-Approval Requirement**: All items intended for reimbursement must be approved by your manager *before* the purchase is made.
* **Submission Deadline**: Reimbursement requests, complete with valid tax invoices, must be submitted to the accounts team at **Accounts@kalagato.co** on or before the **25th of each month**.
* **Processing**: Approved reimbursements will be processed along with the monthly payroll run. Late submissions will be deferred to the following month's cycle.

---

## 4. Performance Review Cycles

We conduct structured reviews to align professional goals, evaluate individual contributions, and reward high performance.

* **Annual Cycle**: Performance reviews are conducted annually in **October**.
* **Deferred Review Matrix**: New hires who have been with the company for **less than 6 months** as of October will have their review deferred to the next annual cycle.
* **Evaluation Criteria**: Individual contributions are evaluated against core business impact, including portfolio-level MRR progress, app stability/ratings, ASO success, architectural efficiency, and proactive domain ownership.
