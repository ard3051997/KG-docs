# Employee Onboarding Manual

Welcome to **KalaGato (Human Data SG Pte. Ltd.)**! We are thrilled to have you join our team. 

At KalaGato, you will be working on products that touch **millions of users** worldwide. As a small, highly specialized team managing a substantial portfolio of **15+ active applications**, your contribution will have direct, visible, and immediate impact.

This onboarding guide is designed to get you configured, aligned, and shipping within your first 30 days.

---

## Your First 30 Days

We believe in structured, progressive integration to help you build confidence and context without feeling overwhelmed.

### Week 1: Core Setup & Context Gathering
* **Step 1: Access Provisioning**: Ensure you have credentials and accounts set up for all systems (see the [Setup Checklist](#access--setup-checklist) below).
* **Step 2: Business & Portfolio Review**: Spend time exploring the active portfolio, reading through our apps, analyzing store performance, and reviewing historical metrics.
* **Step 3: Tech/Design/ASO Setup**: Configure your local workstation. Clone active repositories, explore the asset libraries, and familiarize yourself with existing brand guidelines.
* **Step 4: Team Shadowing**: Shadow your immediate team lead for a full day to observe active communication patterns, Jira tracking, and workflow management.

### Week 2: Initial Task & Exploration
* **Step 1: Introduce Yourself**: Join our Monday Weekly Sync, meet the team, and share your background.
* **Step 2: The "First Ticket"**: Pick up your first small, low-risk ticket (e.g., a simple bug fix, a copy change, a localized keyword update, or a basic creative asset design).
* **Step 3: Standard Methodology Study**: Read our specialized internal playbooks, including the *Inverse Pyramid ASO* methodology and current product roadmaps.
* **Step 4: Engage & Ask**: Use the Slack channels to ask questions. We foster a highly supportive, open communication environment.

### Weeks 34: Execution & Goals Alignment
* **Step 1: Deliver Your First Core Task**: Complete and ship your first substantial deliverable (e.g., a full feature, a coordinated design sprint, or a performance marketing asset package).
* **Step 2: Share Learnings**: Post a brief update in Notion summarizing what you learned, what you shipped, and where you encountered friction.
* **Step 3: Strategic 1:1**: Schedule a 90-day goals alignment sync with the Product Lead to define your primary KPIs.
* **Step 4: Triage Engagement**: Join our active app review triage routines to build direct empathy with our users.

## Leave & Errand Processes

We respect your personal time and operate a highly flexible, async-friendly leave policy. The key is early and clear communication.

### Time-Off Allocations
1. **Casual Leave (12 days/year)**: For personal errands, errands, and short breaks. A maximum of 3 consecutive casual leave days can be taken at a time.
2. **Sick Leave (6 days/year)**: For illness or medical emergencies. Simply notify the team on the day itself before 9:00 AM.
3. **Public Holidays**: Observed in accordance with the official government calendar.

### How to Apply for Planned Leave
1. **Slack Notification**: Message your immediate team lead on Slack with the requested dates and a brief reason.
2. **Acknowledge**: Your lead will give a confirmation reply (e.g., thumbs-up or brief approval).
3. **Calendar Entry**: Add the dates to the shared **Kalagato Leaves** Google Calendar.
4. **Razorpay Update**: Record the request in the Razorpay portal for formal compliance.
5. **Lead Time**: For planned time-off, please notify your lead at least **7 working days** in advance.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Operational Coverage**: Because we operate as a highly focused, lean team, coverage is crucial. Always coordinate with your department colleagues to ensure your active projects and weekly deliverables are cleanly handed off before your leave starts.
