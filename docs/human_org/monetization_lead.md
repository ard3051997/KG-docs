# Product Marketer & Monetization Lead

The **Product Marketer & Monetization Lead** owns the monetization engines, pricing models, paywall experiments, ad placement optimization, and promotional strategy across the KalaGato portfolio. This role is responsible for maintaining high subscription conversions and maximizing ad revenue while ensuring a delightful user experience.

---

## Core Responsibilities

1. **Monetization Architecture & Strategy**: Crafting monetization briefs in the PRD. Defining where and how ad units (interstitials, native banners, rewarded videos) are integrated.
2. **Paywall & Pricing Experiments**: Designing and executing paywall layouts and pricing models using **RevenueCat** and **Superwall**. Setting floor prices and target discounts.
3. **User Communication & Conversion**: Formulating copy and visual briefs for high-impact push notifications (PNs) and in-app message campaigns to drive subscriptions.
4. **Cross-Department Collaboration**:
 * Partnering with **Design** to layout paywalls and position ad units in compliance with app store guidelines (e.g. avoiding excessive interstitial popups).
 * Partnering with **Engineering** to integrate ad network SDKs and establish proper floor pricing setups.
 * Partnering with the **Product Lead** to analyze cohort lifetime value (LTV) and refine monetization roadmaps.

---

## Daily & Weekly Routine

### Daily Checklist
- [ ] **Monetization Telemetry Audit**: Monitor subscription logs in RevenueCat and ad revenue metrics in AdMob.
- [ ] **Campaign Performance Check**: Track push notification open rates and active conversion funnels.
- [ ] **Ad Floor Monitoring**: Verify that ad networks are serving impressions properly and floor prices are healthy.

### Weekly Checklist
- [ ] **Paywall Cohort Analysis**: Deep-dive into active paywall A/B experiments and optimize visual elements.
- [ ] **Monday Weekly Sync**: Coordinate with PMs and Developers on monetization roadmap deliverables and blockers.
- [ ] **Ad Mediation Tuning**: Adjust mediation groups, bid parameters, and ad formats to improve portfolio-wide margins.

---

## Mandatory Approval Gates & Policies

The Monetization Lead enforces the following structural guardrails:

| Category | Policy / Constraint | Required Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Ad Placement Hygiene** | Interstitial placement bounds (e.g. no interstitial immediately following a button tap, or consecutive popups) | Technical review with PM and QA during PRD stage |
| **Floor Pricing Mod** | Changing ad floor parameters in AdMob or networks | Document baseline impact and record transition parameters |
| **Subscription Discounts** | Staging new pricing models or discount rules | Align with Product Lead and verify RevenueCat sandbox tests |
| **Monetization SDKs** | Integrating new mediation libraries or analytical SDKs | Partner with Tech Lead for performance and package size audit |
