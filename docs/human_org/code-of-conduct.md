# Code of Conduct & Ethics Policy

This document defines the behavioral expectations, ethical guidelines, and professional standards for every individual working at **KalaGato (Human Data SG Pte. Ltd.)**. It applies to all employees, contractors, consultants, and temporary workers regardless of location, seniority, or engagement type.

---

## Purpose

KalaGato builds and operates mobile applications used by **millions of people worldwide**. The trust our users place in our products, the integrity of our data practices, and the quality of our workplace culture are non-negotiable foundations. This policy exists to:

1. Establish clear, enforceable expectations for professional behavior.
2. Protect employees, users, partners, and the company from harm.
3. Create a safe, inclusive, and productive working environment.
4. Ensure compliance with applicable laws in Singapore, India, and all markets where our applications operate.

---

## Core Principles

### Integrity in All Actions
Every employee is expected to act honestly, transparently, and ethically in all business dealings. This includes interactions with colleagues, users, vendors, partners, app store representatives, and regulatory bodies. If a situation feels ethically ambiguous, escalate it to your manager or the Founder before proceeding.

### User Trust is Sacred
Our applications handle sensitive user data including browsing activity (VPN products), financial calculations (EMI/GST calculators), voice recordings (Voice Changer), and personal communications. Every employee must treat user data with the highest possible level of care and never access, export, or share user information outside of documented, approved workflows.

### Meritocratic Culture
Decisions about hiring, promotion, compensation, and project assignments are made based on demonstrated competence, impact, and alignment with company values. Discrimination based on race, gender, religion, caste, nationality, sexual orientation, age, disability, or any other protected characteristic is strictly prohibited.

---

## Workplace Conduct Standards

### Professional Communication
1. **Respectful Discourse**: All communication on Slack, Jira, Notion, email, and in meetings must be professional and constructive. Disagreement is welcome and encouraged; personal attacks, passive-aggression, and dismissive language are not.
2. **Feedback Culture**: Provide feedback that is specific, actionable, and focused on work product rather than personal attributes. Accept feedback with openness and a growth mindset.
3. **Language Standards**: English is the primary language for all documentation, code comments, Jira tickets, and formal communication. Regional languages may be used in informal conversations, but all decision-relevant communication must be documented in English for organizational transparency.

### Anti-Harassment & Anti-Discrimination
KalaGato maintains a zero-tolerance policy toward:
- Sexual harassment of any kind (verbal, physical, visual, or digital).
- Bullying, intimidation, or creating a hostile work environment.
- Discrimination in hiring, assignments, or evaluations based on protected characteristics.
- Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith.

**Reporting Mechanism**: Any employee who experiences or witnesses harassment or discrimination should report it directly to the Founder. All reports will be investigated promptly and handled with strict confidentiality. Retaliation against a reporter will result in immediate disciplinary action, up to and including termination.

### Workplace Safety
- Maintain a clean, safe, and professional workspace (physical or remote).
- Report any safety hazards, equipment malfunctions, or security concerns immediately.
- Substance abuse that impairs work performance or judgment is prohibited during working hours.

## Conflict of Interest

### Definition
A conflict of interest arises when an employee's personal interests, relationships, or external activities could compromise (or appear to compromise) their professional judgment or loyalty to KalaGato.

### Common Scenarios
1. **Competing Employment**: Working for, advising, or consulting with a competitor, a mobile app acquisition broker, or an ad network vendor while employed at KalaGato.
2. **Financial Interests**: Holding significant financial interests in a company that KalaGato does business with (ad networks, attribution platforms, app brokerages).
3. **Vendor Relationships**: Having a personal relationship with a vendor, contractor, or M&A counterparty that could influence professional decisions.
4. **Side Projects**: Developing or contributing to mobile applications that compete with KalaGato's portfolio categories (VPN, security, voice modification, translation, calculators, gaming).

### Disclosure Requirement
All potential conflicts of interest must be disclosed in writing to the Founder **before** the conflicting activity begins. Undisclosed conflicts discovered after the fact will be treated as a serious policy violation.

## Use of Company Resources

### Acceptable Use
Company resources including hardware (laptops, phones, tablets), software licenses, cloud infrastructure, developer console access, and ad network accounts are provided exclusively for KalaGato business purposes.

### Prohibited Use
- Using company developer accounts or console credentials for personal app publishing.
- Using company ad network accounts (AdMob, Google Ads, Apple Search Ads) for personal projects.
- Mining or exporting user data for external use.
- Installing unauthorized software or connecting unauthorized devices to company systems.
- Using company cloud infrastructure for cryptocurrency mining, personal hosting, or non-business computation.

### Device Security
All devices used to access KalaGato systems must meet minimum security standards per the Information Security & Data Privacy Policy.

## External Communications & Public Representation

### Social Media & Public Forums
1. Employees must not disclose proprietary business metrics (MRR, user counts, conversion rates, ad revenue figures) on social media, public forums, podcasts, or conference presentations without explicit written approval from the Founder.
2. When posting about the mobile app industry, employees should include a disclaimer that views are personal and do not represent KalaGato.
3. Do not share internal Slack conversations, Jira tickets, Notion pages, or internal metrics screenshots publicly.

### Press & Media
All media inquiries, interview requests, and press communications must be routed through the Founder. No employee is authorized to speak on behalf of KalaGato to journalists, bloggers, or industry analysts without prior approval.

### App Store Communications
Interactions with Apple App Review, Google Play Policy teams, or store representatives must be coordinated through the designated console administrators. Unauthorized communications with store teams can create compliance risks.

## Gifts, Entertainment & Anti-Bribery

### Policy
KalaGato prohibits bribery, kickbacks, and improper payments in all forms. This applies to interactions with app store representatives, ad network partners, M&A brokers, government officials, and any other external parties.

### Gift Guidelines
- **Receiving**: Employees may accept modest business courtesies (meals, branded merchandise) valued under $50 USD equivalent. Anything above this threshold must be disclosed and approved by the Founder.
- **Giving**: Business gifts or entertainment offered to external parties must be reasonable, lawful, and approved by the Founder if the value exceeds $100 USD equivalent.
- **Prohibited**: Cash gifts, gift cards, or any form of payment to external parties as inducement for business advantage.

## Disciplinary Framework

Violations of this Code of Conduct will be addressed through a progressive disciplinary process, except in cases of gross misconduct which may warrant immediate termination.

| Severity | Examples | Action |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Level 1 -- Verbal Warning** | Minor communication issues, first-time tardiness patterns, minor resource misuse | Documented verbal conversation with manager |
| **Level 2 -- Written Warning** | Repeated Level 1 violations, undisclosed minor conflicts, policy non-compliance | Formal written notice with improvement timeline |
| **Level 3 -- Final Warning** | Significant policy violations, data handling negligence, insubordination | Final written warning with Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) |
| **Level 4 -- Termination** | Harassment, fraud, theft, deliberate data breach, gross negligence, bribery | Immediate termination with cause |
