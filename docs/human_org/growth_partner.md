# M&A Analyst & Growth Partner

The **M&A Analyst & Growth Partner** leads our mergers and acquisitions (M&A) pipeline, conducts financial modeling, performs due diligence, and manages post-acquisition portfolio health tracking. This role is crucial to our inorganic growth strategy, leveraging our **$500,000+ acquisition fund** to buy and scale high-potential utility, VPN, and entertainment applications.

---

## Core Responsibilities

1. **Acquisition Sourcing**: Proactively scouring listing platforms, brokerages, and direct developer databases to discover mobile apps that fit our criteria.
2. **Financial Modeling & Valuation**: Building comprehensive LTV, churn, MRR, and margin models to value target apps. Our acquisition sweet spot is **$50,000$200,000** deal size targeting stable profit multiples between **1.5x and 2x**.
3. **Comprehensive Due Diligence**: Coordinates cross-functional reviews of candidate apps:
 * **Financial Diligence**: Auditing billing records, AdMob revenue logs, and RevenueCat history.
 * **Technical Diligence**: Working with the **Tech Lead** to audit codebase stability, structural integrity, and third-party libraries.
 * **Growth Diligence**: Partnering with the **ASO Lead** to analyze current rankings, keyword slots, and organic-to-paid ratios.
4. **Post-Acquisition Integration**: Tracking post-deal health, managing asset transitions (transferring accounts, console credentials, SDK tokens), and coordinating initial growth pushes.

---

## Weekly & Monthly Routine

### Weekly Tasks
* **Pipeline Scrapes & Audits**: Discover and screen 510 new candidate mobile apps using AppFollow, SensorTower, and developer outreach.
* **Underwriting Sprints**: Update standard financial spreadsheets, calculate payback periods, and map risk factors.
* **Weekly Metric Check**: Review post-acquisition revenue trackers to verify that newly acquired apps meet underwriting projections.
* **Monday Weekly Sync**: Share pipeline updates, highlight top targets, and flag active deal status to the full team.

### Monthly Tasks
* **Portfolio Integration Reviews**: Conduct deep-dive syncs with the **Product Lead** on acquired apps' monetization performance.
* **Deal Structuring**: Frame APA structures, earn-out parameters, and transitional support agreements for active targets.
* **Industry Valuation Audits**: Calibrate multipliers and target niches based on active broker valuations and platform changes.

---

## Key Department Relationships

* **Founder & CEO**: Review high-level target strategy and obtain formal deal approvals.
* **Tech Lead**: Partner for technical codebase reviews and structural audits.
* **ASO Lead**: Partner for organic search rankings audit and keyword potential reviews.
* **Product Lead**: Align on asset transitions, feature backlog integration, and conversion optimization plans.

## Mandatory Gates & Limits

| Operational Boundary | Operational Threshold | Required Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Pipeline Escalation** | Elevating a target from "Scouting" to "Active Underwriting" | Verify core metrics meet 1.52x multiple profile |
