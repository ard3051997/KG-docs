# APM & ASO Lead

The **APM & ASO Lead** owns our global App Store Optimization (ASO) strategy, paid user acquisition (UA) budget allocations, keyword slot management, and marketing creative direction across our portfolio of 15+ live applications. This role focuses on driving high-impact organic conversions and optimizing ad investments to maximize our Return on Ad Spend (RoAS).

---

## Core Responsibilities

1. **Global ASO Strategy**: Creating, executing, and optimizing metadata, localizations, and app store copy to improve organic rankings by country.
2. **ASO Keyword Slot Management**: Auditing and updating localized keyword baskets using our specialized *ASO Keyword Slot Manager React dashboard*.
3. **Paid UA Budget Allocation**: Planning performance marketing campaigns (Google Ads, Apple Search Ads) utilizing our **$200,000/month credit line**. Monitoring ROI and managing paid ad distributions.
4. **M&A Growth Due Diligence**: Performing rigorous ASO and organic growth auditing on prospective acquisition targets pre-deal.
5. **Creative Collaboration**: Working directly with the **Design** team to draft, test, and localize high-converting app store screenshots, video previews, and graphic elements.

---

## Daily & Weekly Routine

### Daily Checklist
- [ ] **ASO Rank Triage**: Check daily keyword movements and store category rankings.
- [ ] **Paid Campaign Efficacy Audit**: Monitor Google Ads and Apple Search Ads performance to ensure RoAS is within defined parameters.
- [ ] **Jira Updates**: Post keyword slot alterations and UA metrics to corresponding active sprint tickets.

### Weekly Checklist
- [ ] **Ad Spend Optimization**: Re-allocate paid UA budgets across the portfolio, ensuring zero organic traffic cannibalization.
- [ ] **Monday Weekly Sync**: Present ASO achievements, ranking updates, and coordinate screenshots testing timelines.
- [ ] **ASO Target Reviews**: Provide organic traffic appraisals for newly sourced app targets to the M&A analyst.

## Mandatory Approval Gates & Bounds

The ASO & Paid UA Lead governs the following operational limits:

| Category | Budget Boundary | Required Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **UA Budget Expansion** | Any paid channel expansion exceeding **$500/day** | Prepare RoAS analysis and coordinate approval with CEO |
| **Asset Metadata Updates** | Uploading new keyword configurations or descriptions | Cross-check localization parameters and verify in Jira |
| **Creative Testing** | Running live Google Play or App Store Connect A/B screenshot tests | Confirm brand compliance and secure PM alignment |
| **Ad Credential Allocations** | Provisioning user access to Google Ads or analytics consoles | Obtain Tech Lead sign-off for console security verification |
